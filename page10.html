How to use the booking tool

Our apartments are rented on a nightly basis

The price depends on the season. In low season, from October to April, the price per night is lower. In high season, from May to September, the price per night is higher.

Your reservation request will only be confirmed once we have received the deposit.

Once you have made your reservation request, you will receive three emails from us: the first to inform you that we have received your request, the second to confirm your reservation with an invoice for the deposit, and a third email to give you all the necessary information about key collection on the day of your arrival.
